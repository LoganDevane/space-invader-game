HOW TO PLAY

🎮 CONTROLS

  • ← / A — Move Left
  • → / D — Move Right
  • SPACE — Shoot
  • P — Pause

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Defend Earth as Mark Grayson (Invincible) against waves of Viltrumite invaders! Destroy all enemies to advance through 3 increasingly difficult levels.

⚠️ LEVELS

  • Level 1: Viltrumite Scouts — Standard enemies
  • Level 2: Viltrumite Warriors — Faster, more numerous, they shoot back
  • Level 3: Omni-Man Boss Battle — Face the ultimate Viltrumite

❤️ LIVES & SCORING

You start with 3 lives. Earn points for each enemy destroyed. Bonus points for completing levels quickly!
